Most of what you have read about AI search was measured wrong

Not by a little. The headline results everyone quotes come from experiments where the page was already inside the model's context window. They measure how a model picks between sources it has already retrieved. They say nothing about getting retrieved.

+41%
The famous gain from adding quotations, from the foundational GEO paper. Measured on documents already placed in a five-document context. The paper does not establish organic discoverability, and does not measure traffic. Aggarwal et al., KDD 2024
3 / 54
Method-and-domain combinations that were significantly positive when the published GEO techniques were independently benchmarked. None in question answering. Under broad adoption the gains approach zero, because one source's gain is another's loss. C-SEO Bench, Puerto et al. 2025
−9%
What happened to top-20 presence in the one study that measured the whole pipeline instead of a fixed context. Optimizing the body text made things worse, because rewriting for quotability degrades the topical signals that get a page retrieved at all. SAGEO Arena, Kim et al. 2026
0.34
Day-to-day source overlap across four engines over 45 days, at the low end. Repeated runs at temperature zero changed 9–28% of decisions. A single before-and-after screenshot proves nothing. Schulte et al. 2026; Kirsten et al. 2026

There are no additional requirements to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode, nor other special optimizations necessary.

Seven stages, and you only control the first three

A page has to be reachable, then indexed, then retrieved, then selected, then quoted. A failure at any stage makes every later optimization worthless. Almost all published advice acts on stage five.

  1. Activation

    Does the system search at all? AI Overviews fire on about 13.7% of queries overall, but 64.7% of queries phrased as questions.

  2. Crawling and indexing

    Fetched, rendered, stored. Blocked in robots, client-rendered, or canonicalised away: all terminal.

  3. Retrieval

    In the candidate set for this query. Most of the leverage lives here, and most advice ignores it.

  4. Reranking

    Which candidates reach the top slots or the context window. Position beats almost any rewrite.

  5. Citation

    Does the answer use your page, and name you? The only stage the popular literature measures.

  6. Fidelity

    Is what it says about you correct? Only about half of generated sentences are fully supported by their citations.

  7. Behaviour

    Did anyone act? One suggestive quasi-experiment links AI citation to traffic, and its placebo test was not significant.

Every claim carries a tier

This is the discipline the skill enforces on itself and on the agent using it. A recommendation without a tier is an opinion with confidence added.

Applied throughout the reference material. The bottom two rows exist so an agent can refuse a request and give a mechanism rather than a verdict.
TierWhat it meansHow to treat it
Confirmed Documented by the platform owner, or replicated end to end with measured outcomes. Do it. Non-negotiable.
Supported Consistent across independent studies, but correlational or conditional on other stages. Do it, and state the condition.
Plausible Mechanistically sensible, cheap, no direct evidence either way. Only if it costs almost nothing. Never bill it as a driver.
Unsupported Widely repeated, no evidence, or evidence against. Say so out loud. Do not do it.
Harmful Measured negative effect, or a named policy violation. Refuse, and explain the mechanism of harm.

Two scripts, no dependencies

audit-page.mjs reads built HTML or a live URL and reports what is machine-checkable, ranked by severity. It exits non-zero on any error, so it works as a deploy gate. structured-data.mjs builds one connected JSON-LD graph from a business file and refuses the markup patterns that trigger manual actions.

What it will not do

Promise rankings

Nobody controls them. It promises the fixes, the measurement, and the cadence, and it states the realistic timeline: long tail in months two to three, competitive terms in months three to six.

Write copy for a real business

You do not know their prices, guarantees, or service radius. It builds the structure, marks the gaps with visible placeholders, and produces a brief telling the owner exactly what to write.

Claim an unmeasured win

Run the audit before, run it after, state the delta. "This should improve rankings" is banned in the skill's own rules for the agent using it.
