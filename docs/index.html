A staged model of search retrieval, and an agent skill that implements it

Why most published guidance on AI search optimises the wrong stage, and what to do instead.

visibility — open-source agent skill Marco Zambrella · July 2026 · MIT licensed github.com/marcozambrella/visibility-skill

Abstract

Being found is a pipeline of seven dependent stages, from whether a system searches at all through to whether anyone acts on the answer. A failure at any stage makes every later optimisation worthless. Nearly all published guidance on generative search acts on the fifth stage, and the evidence for those interventions is conditional on the document having already been retrieved — a condition the source studies fix by construction and never establish.

This skill reorganises the problem around the pipeline, attaches an explicit evidence tier to every recommendation, and separates what an agent can do from what only a person can. It ships two dependency-free scripts: a static audit that runs as a deploy gate, and a JSON-LD graph builder that refuses the markup patterns known to trigger manual actions.

1Availability

Works with Claude Code and any runtime that reads SKILL.md. The reference material is plain Markdown and is useful on its own.

2The retrieval pipeline

A page has to be reachable, then indexed, then retrieved for a query, then selected over competitors, then rendered so a human or a model can lift an answer from it. The stages are dependent, not additive. Figure 1 shows where the attrition falls and how much of it is under a site owner's control.

  1. Activation.

    Does the system search at all? AI Overviews fire on roughly 13.7% of queries overall, rising to about 64.7% of queries phrased as questions [5].

  2. Crawling and indexing.

    Fetched, rendered, stored. Blocked in robots, client-rendered, or canonicalised away: all terminal.

  3. Retrieval.

    Present in the candidate set for this query. Most of the leverage lives here, and most guidance ignores it.

  4. Reranking and context allocation.

    Which candidates reach the top slots or the model's context window. Position beats almost any rewrite.

  5. Generation and citation.

    Does the answer use the page, and name it? The only stage the popular literature measures.

  6. Absorption and fidelity.

    Is what the answer says correct? Roughly half of generated sentences were fully supported by their citations in the sources surveyed [5].

  7. Attention, click, conversion.

    Did anyone act? One suggestive quasi-experiment links AI citation to traffic, and its placebo test was not significant [5].

The decomposition that follows is the practical use of the model. If a page is not retrieved, work on stage three; rewriting its paragraphs to sound more quotable is stage-five work, and stage-five work on an unretrieved page has an expected value of zero.

3What the evidence supports

The results most often quoted in support of generative engine optimisation come from experiments in which the candidate document was already placed in the model's context window. They measure how a model chooses between sources it has already retrieved. Table 1 collects the four findings that most change what a practitioner should do.

The platform's own position is consistent with the replication record, and is worth quoting to anyone who has been sold an AI-readiness package.

There are no additional requirements to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode, nor other special optimizations necessary.

Google Search Central, AI features documentation [6]

Three findings did replicate: topical relevance, described as the most reproducible factor; position in the ranking or context window, whose effect exceeds that of most rewrites; and extractable evidence — statistics, dates, definitions, quotations — conditional on retrieval having already occurred [5].

4Evidence tiers

Every recommendation in the skill carries one of five labels. The discipline is the point: a recommendation without a tier is an opinion with confidence added.

5Method, applied

What the skill does to a page, in the order it does it. The case below is a single-page site for a home renovation company: eight services, one title tag, no domain configured, no structured data worth the name.

5.1Establishing a baseline

The first command is the audit, and its output is the baseline. Nothing is called an improvement later unless these counts moved.

5.2One page becomes nine

The finding that produces growth, and the one no audit tool reports, because the failure is an absence. Eight services on one page means one title, one heading, one topic — and nothing that can rank for any of the eight things people type.

5.3Making a passage retrievable

Retrieval works over passages, and the heading states what the passage under it answers. Investment is retrievable for nothing; the question people type is retrievable for exactly itself.

5.4Resolving the business as an entity

One file describing the business, one command, one connected graph with stable identifiers — instead of the disconnected blocks most sites ship.

7Limitations

Stated plainly, because a practitioner told the limits trusts the numbers that are reported.

It does not promise rankings

Nobody controls them. It promises the fixes, the measurement, and the cadence, and it states the realistic timeline: long-tail movement in months two to three, competitive terms in months three to six.

It does not write copy for a real business

An agent does not know a client's prices, guarantees or service radius. The skill builds the structure, marks the gaps with visible placeholders, and produces a brief telling the owner exactly what to write. Invented copy has to be undone, and distorts the architecture while it exists.

Several quantities are not measurable with current tools

Assistant referral attribution is incomplete, since many clicks arrive with no referrer. AI citation rates are noisy enough to require repetition to distinguish from generation variance [4]. The link between an AI citation and revenue rests on one quasi-experiment whose placebo test was not significant [5].

Gains erode under adoption

The setting is competitive, and one source's gain is another's loss [2]. Durable advantage comes from what a competitor cannot copy in an afternoon: real photographs, real reviews, real expertise, real relationships.
